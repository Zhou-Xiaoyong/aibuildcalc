How to Use This Calculator

Siding replacement costs depend on material, style, and whether old siding needs removal. This calculator uses 2026 national cost data to estimate your total project cost.

  1. Enter the total exterior wall area in square feet
  2. Select the siding material type
  3. Choose the siding style (horizontal, vertical, or shakes)
  4. Indicate whether old siding needs to be removed
  5. Select your regional cost factor
Expert Insights Smart analysis for your siding project
🏡
Fiber Cement = Best Value
Fiber cement (HardiePlank) costs ~40% more than vinyl but lasts 2-3x longer (25-50 years). It's fire-resistant, insect-proof, and one of the best ROI siding choices.
💰
High Curb Appeal ROI
Siding replacement returns 70-85% of cost at resale — one of the highest-ROI exterior improvements. Fiber cement and stone veneer add the most perceived home value.
🔍
Don't Skip Inspection
Removing old siding often reveals water damage, rot, or mold. Budget an extra 10-15% contingency for repairs to the wall sheathing and flashing before installing new siding.

Calculation Methodology

Base Cost = Wall Area x Cost per Sq Ft (by type) Style Multiplier: Horizontal x1.0, Vertical x1.15, Shakes x1.25 Removal = Wall Area x $2.00/sq ft (if selected) Adjusted = (Base x Style + Removal) x Location Factor Materials = Adjusted x 0.60, Labor = Adjusted x 0.40 Total = Adjusted

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to replace siding?
The national average siding replacement cost is $6,000-$18,000 for a 1,500 sq ft home. Vinyl siding costs $3-$8 per sq ft, fiber cement $5-$12, wood $5-$10, aluminum $3-$7, stucco $6-$12, brick veneer $8-$15, and stone veneer $10-$25 per sq ft installed.
What is the cheapest siding?
Vinyl is the cheapest siding at $3-$8 per square foot installed. Aluminum is also affordable at $3-$7 per sq ft. Wood costs $5-$10, fiber cement (Hardie) $5-$12, stucco $6-$12, brick veneer $8-$15, and stone veneer $10-$25 per sq ft.
How long does siding last?
Vinyl: 20-40 years. Fiber cement: 25-50 years. Wood: 20-40 years (with maintenance). Aluminum: 20-30 years. Stucco: 50-80 years. Brick: 100+ years. Stone veneer: 50-75 years. Fiber cement and stucco offer the best durability-to-cost ratio.

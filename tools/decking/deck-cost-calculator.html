How to Use This Calculator

Deck costs vary significantly by material, railing type, and extras. This calculator uses 2026 national cost data to estimate your total deck building cost.

  2. Select the decking material

Expert Insights Smart analysis for your deck project
🌿
Composite = Low Maintenance
Composite decking costs ~50% more than wood but never needs staining. Over 25 years, composite saves $1,000-$3,000 in maintenance and lasts 2-3x longer than wood.
💰
High Outdoor ROI
Decks return 65-75% of cost at resale — one of the best outdoor investments. A 200 sq ft composite deck can recoup $6,000-$10,000 when you sell.
⚙️
Railing Costs Add Up
Railings account for 25-40% of total deck cost. Aluminum and glass/cable railings look modern but cost 2-3x more than basic wood railings.

Calculation Methodology

Decking Cost = Area x Cost per Sq Ft (by material) Perimeter ≈ 4 x sqrt(Area) (square deck approximation) Railing Cost = Perimeter x Railing Cost per Ft Stairs = $450 per set Features: Bench $350, Planter $250, Both $550 Total = (Decking + Railing + Stairs + Features) x Location Factor Materials = Total x 0.55, Labor = Total x 0.45

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to build a deck?
The national average cost to build a deck is $15-$45 per square foot installed. A 200 sq ft pressure-treated wood deck costs $3,000-$6,000. Cedar decks cost $5,000-$9,000, composite $8,000-$14,000, and PVC or tropical hardwood $9,000-$18,000 for 200 sq ft.
What is the cheapest deck material?
Pressure-treated pine is the cheapest decking material at $15-$25 per square foot installed. Cedar costs $25-$35 per sq ft. Composite (Trex, TimberTech) costs $30-$45 per sq ft. PVC and tropical hardwoods (ipe, cumaru) are premium at $35-$60 per sq ft.
Does a deck add value to a home?
Yes. A wood deck addition returns about 65-75% of cost at resale, while a composite deck returns 60-70%. Decks are one of the best outdoor improvements for ROI, especially in moderate climates where they can be used year-round.

How to Use This Calculator

Roof replacement costs depend heavily on material choice, roof pitch, tear-off needs, and your location. This calculator uses 2026 national cost data adjusted for regional labor rates.

  1. Enter your roof footprint in square feet (use the ground-level area of your home)
  2. Select the roofing material you want installed
  3. Choose your roof pitch — steeper roofs cost more to install
  4. Select the number of stories (affects labor difficulty)
  5. Indicate whether old roofing needs to be torn off
  6. Choose your regional cost factor
Expert Insights Smart analysis for your roof project
🏠
Pitch Multiplies Material
Roof pitch increases actual surface area above the footprint. A 1,800 sq ft home with a steep 12/12 pitch needs ~2,430 sq ft of roofing material.
💰
Material Lifespan ROI
Asphalt lasts 15-25 years. Metal lasts 40-70 years. Slate lasts 75-150 years. The higher upfront cost of premium materials often pays off in fewer replacements.
⚠️
Overlay Limit
Most building codes allow only 2 layers of shingles. If you already have 2 layers, a tear-off is required. Overlaying also hides deck damage and shortens roof life.

Calculation Methodology

Actual Roof Area = Footprint x Pitch Factor Material Cost = Actual Area x Material Price per Sq Ft Labor & Install = Material Cost x 0.60 (labor ~40% of total installed) Tear-Off = Actual Area x $1.50-$3.00/sq ft (if selected) Location Adjustment = Subtotal x Location Factor Stories Factor = Subtotal x Stories Multiplier Total = Material + Labor + Tear-Off, adjusted for location and stories

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to replace a roof?
The national average roof replacement cost is $8,500 for asphalt shingles on a 2,000 sq ft home, with most projects ranging from $5,500 to $14,000. Metal roofs cost $12,000-$24,000, tile $15,000-$30,000, and slate $25,000-$50,000.
How much does a new roof cost per square foot?
Asphalt shingles: $4.50-$8.00 per sq ft installed. Metal: $7-$16 per sq ft. Tile: $12-$20 per sq ft. Slate: $18-$30 per sq ft. Flat (EPDM/TPO): $6-$12 per sq ft. Steeper roofs and multi-story homes add 10-35%.
Does roof pitch affect cost?
Yes. Steeper roofs require more materials and are more dangerous to install. A steep roof (9/12 or higher) costs roughly 35% more than a flat or low-pitch roof for the same footprint.

How to Use This Calculator

Bathroom remodel costs vary widely based on the scope of work, whether you change the plumbing layout, and your location. This calculator uses 2026 national cost data adjusted for regional labor rates.

  1. Enter the bathroom's total square footage
  2. Select the remodel scope — cosmetic, mid-grade, full gut, or luxury
  3. Indicate whether you plan to change the plumbing layout
  4. Choose whether demolition of existing finishes is included
  5. Select your regional cost factor
Expert Insights Smart analysis for your bathroom budget
🚿
Layout Changes Cost Big
Moving drain lines and supply pipes adds 10-25% to your total. Keeping the existing plumbing layout is the single biggest way to stay on budget.
💰
Bathroom ROI
Mid-range bathroom remodels return about 60-70% of cost at resale. Upscale remodels return closer to 50-60%. Focus on fixtures, tile, and lighting for best value.
⚠️
Mold & Water Damage
Older bathrooms often hide mold or water damage behind tile. Budget an extra 10-15% contingency for repairs discovered during demolition.

Calculation Methodology

Base Cost = Bathroom Size x Cost per Sq Ft (by scope) Plumbing Factor: None x1.0, Minor x1.1, Major x1.25 Demolition = Size x $5-$8/sq ft (if selected) Adjusted Cost = (Base x Plumbing Factor + Demolition) x Location Factor Labor & Plumbing = Adjusted Cost x 0.50 Fixtures & Materials = Adjusted Cost x 0.50 Total = Adjusted Cost

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?
The national average bathroom remodel costs $10,000-$25,000 for a mid-grade renovation of a full bath. Cosmetic refreshes cost $3,000-$7,000, while full gut renovations cost $20,000-$50,000+. Primary bathrooms with luxury finishes can exceed $60,000.
How much does a bathroom remodel cost per square foot?
Cosmetic refresh: $50-$100 per sq ft. Mid-grade remodel: $150-$250 per sq ft. Full gut renovation: $250-$400 per sq ft. Luxury primary bathrooms: $400-$700+ per sq ft. Plumbing layout changes add 10-25%.
What is the most expensive part of a bathroom remodel?
Labor and plumbing are typically the largest costs at 40-50% of the total. Fixtures (vanity, toilet, tub/shower) account for 20-30%, tile and flooring 15-20%, and electrical/drywall 10-15%.

How Much Does It Cost to Install Vinyl Plank Flooring

Published November 2026 · 8 min read · By AIBuildCalc

A 1,150 sq ft first floor in 20 mil click plank books four crew days and lands at $11,102 with the carpet hauled off. The same floor over a clean subfloor quotes at $6,635. Nothing about the plank changed.

Quick answer: vinyl plank runs $3 to $13 per square foot installed in 2026, and most homes land at $4.50 to $6.50 for a lay-only job over a sound subfloor. Labor alone is $2.00 to $4.50 a square foot, or $1,000 to $1,400 a day for a two-man crew that sets 450 to 600 sq ft of click plank. Tear-out, leveling and stairs are separate lines. The Vinyl Flooring Calculator turns dimensions into cartons and material cost.

A crew day is the unit that sets the price

Two men lay 450 to 600 sq ft of floating click plank in eight hours on a clear floor. Glue-down runs 250 to 350, since every plank gets adhesive and a roller. Herringbone drops to 150 to 200. At 575 sq ft a day, a 1,150 sq ft open plan is two lay days and four crew days once tear-out and prep are added.

Labor = crew days x day rate Day rate, two-man crew = $1,000 - $1,400 1,150 sq ft, floating click: 1,150 / 575 per day = 2 lay days 2 x $1,150 = $2,300 for the plank $2,300 / 1,150 = $2.00 per sq ft Stairs, per tread = $45 - $85 Transitions, each = $45 - $120 Minimum job under 300 sq ft = $500 - $900

The helper's wage is the line a cheap bid hides. A lead alone sets 250 sq ft a day, and a helper at $18 to $22 an hour doubles that for 40 percent more money.

Glue-down costs more in labor and buys a quiet floor with no expansion joint at the doorway. Removing it later takes a scraper and a day.

Tear-out is priced by what is on the floor

Removal runs $0.60 to $4.50 a square foot by material. Carpet is $0.60 to $1.25, and the tack strip plus the staples under the pad take longer than the carpet did. Sheet vinyl is $0.75 to $1.50, laminate $1.00 to $2.00, ceramic tile $2.50 to $4.50 because thinset has to be chipped off the slab, hardwood $1.50 to $3.00 for the nails.

Hauling runs $150 to $350 a truck load, or $450 to $600 for a 15-yard dumpster, which swallows 1,000 to 1,200 sq ft of carpet and pad.

Prep is where one bid splits from another

Manufacturers spec the substrate flat within 3/16 in over 10 ft. A 1/8 in hump under a floating floor becomes a rocking seam inside a season, and a broken locking tab is not a warranty claim.

Self-leveler at $1.25 to $3.00 a square foot covers the low spots; 180 sq ft is half a day and $430 in material. On a slab, an ASTM F2170 humidity test runs $150 to $400 and takes 72 hours, and a reading above 75 to 80 percent needs a vapor retarder or a different adhesive.

Over wood, check deflection first. A floor that moves under a heel needs joist work, and the Floor Joist Calculator shows whether a span holds L/360.

Four days on a single-story ranch with carpet throughout, crew of two at $1,150.

Add 51 cartons at $85, which covers 1,265 sq ft at 10 percent waste ($4,335), 240 linear ft of baseboard reset at $2.25 ($540), six transitions at $65 ($390), the leveler at $432 and the moisture test at $325. The invoice reads $11,102, or $9.65 a square foot. Without the tear-out and prep it is $6,635.

Where the rate moves by region

Labor only, 2026: Cleveland $2.25 a square foot, Denver $3.25, Seattle $4.25, San Jose $5.50. On 1,150 sq ft, $2,300 between Cleveland and Seattle.

Stairs, doorways and the small-job premium

A staircase prices by the step. Twelve treads at $45 to $85 each is $540 to $1,020 and a full day. Door undercuts run $25 to $50 a door; a toilet pull and reset $75 to $150. Jobs under 300 sq ft carry a $500 to $900 minimum, which is why a 40 sq ft powder room quotes at $11 a square foot.

If the adjoining rooms are carpet, the Carpet Calculator prices those in square yards on the same visit.

What to book

Book a floating click install when the subfloor is flat and the rooms are separate. Two days and $5.77 a square foot buys a 20 mil plank you can pull up yourself in ten years, with no adhesive to grind.

Book glue-down when the slab is wide open and chairs roll across it all day. It adds about $1.50 a square foot and a moisture test, and the day it comes out you pay for that bond a second time.

Look at the wear layer, not the carton's thickness. A 12 mil floor in a house with two dogs shows a traffic lane by year five. Vinyl carries no AC rating, whatever the box implies. That number belongs to laminate. Every estimator in the flooring collection prices from area, waste and rate.

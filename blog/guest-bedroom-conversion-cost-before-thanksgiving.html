What Does a Guest Bedroom Cost Before Thanksgiving? $1,180 or $16,400

Published November 2026 · 7 min read · By AIBuildCalc

Making the spare room guest-ready before Thanksgiving costs $1,180 when it already has a door, a closet and a window that opens. The same job in a basement with no egress window costs $16,400 and cannot be finished by November 26, because the window is a four-to-six week special order and the permit is another week.

Quick answer: a 12 by 13 spare room runs $1,180: $210 in paint you apply yourself, $830 for carpet installed, $70 for two interconnected smoke and CO alarms, $70 for a blackout panel and rod. A basement bedroom is a different project at $16,400, and $4,200 of it is the egress window. The Home Renovation Cost Calculator prices the scope you are actually planning.

Three things turn a den into a bedroom

A bedroom needs a door that closes, a window a person can climb out of, and a smoke alarm wired to the one in the hall. The closet is the fourth requirement, and it is an appraisal convention rather than a building code one. Skipping the closet does not fail an inspection. Skipping the window does.

That window is where budgets break. IRC R310.2.1 applies to every room used for sleeping: 5.7 sq ft of net clear opening, at least 20 in wide, at least 24 in tall, with the sill no more than 44 in above the finished floor. A second-floor bedroom with a 24 by 36 double hung that lifts 22 in passes by a few inches. A basement slider usually fails outright, and most houses already have the other two pieces.

Carpet is sold by the square yard, so 156 sq ft becomes 19 sq yd once waste is added. At $25 a yard for a mid-grade frieze, $8 for the pad and $10 for the install, that is $43 a yard, or $817. Small rooms carry a minimum, which is why the same carpet in a 400 sq ft room prices nearer $32 a yard. The Carpet Calculator runs the yardage from dimensions.

Paint is the line you can delete the labor from. Two gallons of wall paint at $48, one of ceiling flat at $38, a quart of trim enamel and a roller kit total $210. The bed is not in the $1,180: add $450 for a frame and $600 to $1,800 for a mattress.

The egress window decides the schedule

Net clear opening is not the rough opening, and it is not the glass. On a slider it is roughly half the unit width, because one sash covers the other, and the frame eats two more inches.

Net clear opening = clear width x clear height / 144 32 x 48 single slider, sash overlapping the frame 2 in: clear width = 32 / 2 - 2 = 14 in fails the 20 in minimum clear height = 48 - 4 = 44 in area = 14 x 44 / 144 = 4.3 sq ft fails the 5.7 sq ft minimum 32 x 60 in-swing casement: clear width = 32 - 2 = 30 in clear height = 60 - 2 = 58 in area = 30 x 58 / 144 = 12.1 sq ft passes Sill height, maximum 44 in above the finished floor Window well, minimum 9 sq ft with 36 in of projection Ladder required once the well is deeper than 44 in Carpet waste: 156 sq ft x 1.10 = 172 sq ft = 19 sq yd Wall paint: 360 sq ft x 2 coats / 375 sq ft per gal = 1.9 gal

That arithmetic is why a basement guest room is not a paint-and-carpet job. The cut goes through an eight-inch foundation wall, the well needs drainage at the bottom, and the header over the opening has to carry the block above it. Installed with the well, $2,800 to $6,500, and $4,200 is the common middle for a 32 by 60 casement in block. The Egress Window Calculator checks a unit against all four limits before you order.

Nineteen days, in order

Today is November 7. Guests arrive the 26th, which leaves three working weekends if nothing ships late.

  • This weekend: patch and paint. Walls first, ceiling second, trim last, and a second day before furniture goes against latex.
  • Monday: carpet measured. In-stock goods install in five to ten business days; a special order adds two weeks and does not fit.
  • By the 14th: closet rod and shelf, two alarms, door hardware that actually latches.
  • By the 20th: mattress and frame delivered. A foam bed shipped in a box needs 24 to 72 hours to reach full height, so it cannot arrive on the 25th.
  • The 21st to the 25th: linens, a lamp that works from the bed, a hook on the back of the door.

If the room runs cold once the door shuts, fix that before the mattress arrives. The Thanksgiving heating cost breakdown covers the return-air fix that costs $45 and the ones that cost $1,600.

What not to spend on this month

Skip the accent wall and the window treatments. Nobody remembers either. They remember whether the door closed and whether the room was warm at 2 a.m. Do not start a closet build the week of the holiday, and do not list the room as a bedroom on a rental or resale listing without egress, because the count will not survive an appraiser's tape measure.

Thanksgiving guest room questions

Does a guest room need a closet to count as a bedroom?

The building code does not require one. Appraisers and most listing systems do, and a room without a closet is often recorded as a den. For one holiday, a $60 rod and shelf is enough. For resale, a framed reach-in runs $1,150 to $3,500.

Can I use a basement room as a guest room without an egress window?

You can sleep someone in it. You cannot call it a bedroom, and an inspector will not sign off on one. A compliant opening is $2,800 to $6,500 with the well, so a room without it is a finished basement with a bed in it: fine for an air mattress, wrong for a child.

How much does it cost to add a closet before Thanksgiving?

$450 to $1,150 for a reach-in with a rod, shelf and bifold, and three or four days including paint. A custom system quoted at $2,500 arrives in three weeks, which puts it past the holiday.

The call

Paint the spare room yourself, carpet it, and buy two interconnected combo alarms. That is $1,180 and it is done by the 14th with a week of slack for a late carpet truck.

The trade-off is the closet. If the room has none, a $60 rod and shelf gets you through Thanksgiving and leaves the appraisal argument for spring, when a framed closet can be built without a deadline attached. If the room is in the basement, spend nothing on it this month and book the egress window for January, because a room with a bed in it is not the same asset as a bedroom, and the difference is one hole in a foundation wall.

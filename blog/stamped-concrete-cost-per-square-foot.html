Stamped Concrete Cost Per Square Foot

Published August 2026 · 7 min read · By AIBuildCalc

Stamped concrete gives you the look of stone, brick, or wood plank at a fraction of what those materials cost installed. The trade-off is that it is a labor-intensive finish, and the price spread between a simple one-color stamp and a hand-detailed multi-color job is wide. Understanding which tier you are buying is the single most useful thing you can do before collecting bids.

Quick answer: Stamped concrete typically runs $12 to $28 per square foot installed in 2026, versus roughly $6 to $12 for a plain broom finish. Budget about a $6 to $16 per square foot premium over a plain pour. Size your job with the Stamped Concrete Calculator.

The three pricing tiers

Nearly every stamped concrete quote falls into one of three tiers. Contractors do not always label them this way, but the distinction drives the price:

TierWhat you getInstalled cost per sq ft (2026)
BasicSingle pattern, one integral color, standard release agent$12 - $17
Mid-rangeOne pattern plus a contrasting antiquing release, border band$16 - $22
PremiumMultiple patterns, hand-applied secondary colors, custom borders, saw-cut detail$20 - $28+

Small jobs price higher per square foot because mobilization, forming, and stamp-mat rental do not scale down. A 200 sq ft patio may quote at $20 per square foot while a 900 sq ft pool deck of the same tier quotes at $15.

Stamped vs. plain concrete: the real comparison

The structural slab underneath is identical. Everything above the base cost is finish work, so the comparison is fairly clean:

FactorPlain broom finishStamped concrete
Installed cost per sq ft$6 - $12$12 - $28
400 sq ft patio, total$2,400 - $4,800$4,800 - $11,200
Crew time on site1 - 2 days2 - 4 days
Resealing intervalOptional, 5+ yearsEvery 2 - 3 years, required to hold color
Resealing cost per sq ft$0.50 - $1.25$1.00 - $2.50
Repairs after crackingStraightforward patchDifficult to color-match
Slip resistanceGood as-finishedNeeds a grit additive in the sealer

Over a 20-year horizon, stamped concrete costs meaningfully more than the initial gap suggests, because resealing is not optional if you want the color to survive UV exposure. Factor roughly $0.35 - $0.85 per square foot per year in maintenance.

Estimating your own job

Stamped work is quoted by finished surface area, so the math is simply area times rate. Concrete volume is still calculated the normal way:

Surface area (sq ft) = Length (ft) x Width (ft) Finish cost = Surface area x Tier rate ($/sq ft) Concrete volume (cu yd) = Area x Thickness (in) / 12 / 27 Example: 16 x 25 patio, 4 in thick, mid-range tier Area = 400 sq ft Finish = 400 x $19 = $7,600 Volume = 400 x 0.333 / 27 = 4.9 cu yd (5.4 with 10% waste)

Cost drivers beyond the pattern

  • Color method: integral color mixed at the plant costs more per yard than a broadcast color hardener but wears better over the long run.
  • Borders and bands: a contrasting saw-cut or hand-tooled border typically adds $3 - $7 per linear foot.
  • Site access: if the truck cannot reach the pour, pumping adds roughly $900 - $1,800 for a typical residential job, or wheelbarrow labor adds hours.
  • Substrate condition: overlaying an existing sound slab with a stampable overlay runs $9 - $18 per square foot and avoids demolition entirely.
  • Climate: in freeze-thaw regions the mix must be air-entrained and the sealer breathable, which narrows product choices and adds a little cost.

Where stamped concrete makes financial sense

It competes best against natural stone and pavers, not against plain concrete. Flagstone installed runs $22 - $40 per square foot and brick pavers $15 - $30, so a mid-tier stamp at $19 delivers a similar visual result for less. Against a plain broom pour it is purely a discretionary upgrade. Many homeowners split the difference by stamping only a visible entry area or patio and using a plain finish elsewhere. If you are pricing a brick-look pattern, it is worth checking the real thing with the brick calculator before deciding.

Before you sign

Ask to see a finished job at least three years old from the same crew, confirm the control joint layout in writing, and verify what sealer is included and who pays for the first reseal. Then run your dimensions through the Stamped Concrete Calculator and compare against the per-square-foot tiers above to confirm your bids are in a sane range.
